08Questions

Things people ask us first.

If yours isn't here, WhatsApp us, we reply fast.

Do I need to prepare anything before we start?

No preparation needed. Bring access to your data sources (Excel files, database credentials, spreadsheets or SaaS tool logins) and a clear sense of the decisions you want to make faster. We handle the rest on the discovery call.

How quickly can you deliver a first working version?

Usually within 48 hours of getting your data. Bigger builds that pull from many sources take 5 to 7 working days, and the date goes in writing before we start.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. Everything runs over email, WhatsApp and Zoom, so a business in London or Dubai gets the same process as one in Ahmedabad.

What if my data is messy or incomplete?

That's normal, most business data is. Cleaning and structuring it is a core part of what we do, not an extra. We assess your data in the audit phase and include the cleanup in your scope.

Will my team be able to use the dashboards independently?

That's the goal of every project. Every delivery includes a walkthrough session and written documentation so your team can filter, navigate and interpret the dashboards without calling us.

Is my data kept secure and confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA before any data is shared, work within your own Power BI environment where possible, and never store client data beyond the project scope.

Can you connect to our CRM, Shopify, Google Ads and other tools?

Yes. We connect Power BI to HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, WooCommerce, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, SQL databases, Excel/CSV files, REST APIs and more.
